Birthday Letter for Angel Heart

To my dearest love, Angel Heart Celerinos,

Happy, Happy Birthday, my love! 🎂✨

I wanted to write this letter to tell you, from the absolute bottom of my heart, just how deeply I love you. Looking back at our journey, it's amazing to think that we're almost reaching our 4th year together—ever since our Grade 11 Senior High School days. Through every moment, month, and year that has passed, you have remained my favorite blessing and the absolute best thing that has ever happened to me.

I am endlessly grateful to have you in my life, but I also want to be honest and humble. I know I'm far from being the perfect man or the "man of your dreams." I've made mistakes and done things that hurt you in the past, right from the very beginning of our relationship—and I know I can get a bit jealous at times. From the bottom of my heart, I humbly ask for your forgiveness. Thank you for your endless patience, your understanding, and for loving me despite all my imperfections.

I want to remind you of something very important today: never be ashamed or doubt how you look. To me, outer looks don't define a person, but even so, you are truly beautiful to me in every single way. More than anything, you have a breathtakingly beautiful soul and such a kind, pure heart. That inner beauty is what makes me fall deeper in love with you every single day.

My wish for you on your special day is for even more wonderful and amazing things to enter your life. You deserve all the happiness this world has to offer, and I want you to enjoy every single second of it. Please always remember that I am right here by your side—ready to listen, protect, and support you in all your dreams and endeavors.

Thank you for bringing so much light, warmth, and love into my world, my love. I love you so much, today and always.

Happy Birthday again, my Angel Heart! 💖

Forever yours,
Kyle Geoff A. Cabuhayan
